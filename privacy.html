Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

nest-control is a private, single-user command-line tool. It is operated by its owner to control their own Google Nest thermostat. It has no other users.

Information accessed

When authorized by the owner, nest-control uses Google's Smart Device Management API to read thermostat state (temperature, humidity, mode, HVAC status, and target temperatures) and to send thermostat commands.

How information is handled

Thermostat data is displayed in the owner's terminal and is not stored, logged to any server, analyzed, or transmitted anywhere other than between the owner's own computer and Google's API.

Credentials

OAuth credentials and the refresh token are stored in a file on the owner's own computer, readable only by the owner's user account. They are not shared with anyone.

Third parties

No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party. There are no analytics, advertising, or tracking components. The only external service contacted is Google's own API.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

No thermostat data is retained. Access can be revoked at any time by the owner from their Google Account permissions page, which invalidates the stored token.
